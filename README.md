# 🌍 Global Landslide Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Landslide Analysis** project focuses on analyzing worldwide landslide events to understand their geographical distribution, temporal patterns, triggering factors, and impacts on human lives.

Using **Microsoft Power BI**, I developed an interactive dashboard to explore historical landslide records, identify high-risk geographical areas, analyze fatalities and injuries, and examine patterns that can support disaster preparedness and risk management.

This project demonstrates my skills in data analysis, data visualization, exploratory data analysis, and transforming raw data into meaningful insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Identify countries and geographical regions with the highest number of recorded landslide events.
- Analyze monthly and yearly landslide occurrence patterns.
- Identify the most frequently reported landslide triggers.
- Understand the relationship between landslide categories, sizes, and human impact.
- Analyze fatalities and injuries associated with landslide events.
- Identify geographical regions that require greater attention for disaster preparedness.
- Explore historical trends and develop an exploratory 10-year landslide forecast.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains five analytical sections:

### 1. KPI & Geographical Analysis

- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to summarize landslide events and human impact.
- Geographical distribution of landslide occurrences.
- Country-wise comparison of fatalities and injuries.
- Identification of countries with the highest reported human impact.
- Interactive geographical map for exploring landslide distribution.

### 2. Landslide Impact, Category & Trigger Analysis

- Analysis of landslide events based on their triggering factors.
- Comparison of total landslides and human impact by trigger.
- Analysis of landslide categories and sizes.
- Treemap visualization to understand category-wise distribution.
- Identification of triggers associated with significant human impact.

### 3. Time-Based Analysis

- Monthly distribution of landslide events.
- Yearly trends in landslide occurrences.
- Analysis of fatalities and injuries across different months.
- Combined time-series visualization to compare human impact over time.
- Identification of seasonal and historical patterns.

### 4. Landslide Forecasting

- Exploratory analysis of historical landslide trends.
- Time-series forecasting of landslide occurrences for the next 10 years.
- Visualization of projected trends to support long-term risk awareness and planning.

*Note: Forecast values represent statistical projections based on historical patterns and should not be interpreted as precise predictions of future landslide events.*

### 5. Interactive Dashboard

The report brings together geographical, categorical, temporal, and impact-based visualizations to provide a consolidated view of global landslide patterns.

## 🔍 Key Analytical Areas

The project focuses on four major types of analysis:

**1. Descriptive Analysis**
- Total recorded landslide events.
- Country-wise and region-wise distribution.
- Monthly and yearly event patterns.
- Distribution of landslide triggers and categories.

**2. Diagnostic Analysis**
- Investigation of frequently reported landslide triggers.
- Identification of geographical areas with higher event concentrations.
- Comparison of fatalities and injuries across different triggers.
- Examination of landslide categories and sizes associated with human impact.

**3. Predictive Analysis**
- Historical trend analysis.
- Exploratory time-series forecasting.
- Estimation of future landslide trends over a 10-year horizon.

**4. Prescriptive Analysis**
- Identify geographical areas that may require greater monitoring.
- Support disaster preparedness and early-warning planning.
- Prioritize risk assessment in regions with significant historical human impact.
- Encourage preventive measures in areas experiencing recurring landslide events.
